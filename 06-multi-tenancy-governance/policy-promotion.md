# 7. policy-promotion

## Goal

Promote policies safely across environments.

---

# Recommended Promotion Flow

```text
Development
      ↓
Staging
      ↓
Pre-Production
      ↓
Production
```

---

# Why Important

Policies can:
- block workloads
- break deployments
- affect platform stability

---

# Production Best Practice

Never promote directly to production.

Always:
- validate
- observe
- measure impact

---
